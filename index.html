About KenyaSurvey

KenyaSurvey is a research participation platform that connects eligible participants with survey and data collection projects across Kenya.

We help organizations and researchers gather opinions, demographic data, and survey responses from verified Kenyan participants. Participants who complete eligible surveys may receive compensation via M-Pesa.

Our Mission

To make research participation accessible to all Kenyans while providing fair compensation for participants' time and input.

Compensation Disclaimer

Compensation is not guaranteed. Eligibility depends on project availability, participant demographics, survey completion, and compliance with platform requirements. KenyaSurvey does not promise a specific income level.

Terms of Service

1. Eligibility

You must be 18 years or older and a resident of Kenya to participate. Account registration requires a valid Kenyan mobile number.

2. Survey Participation

Survey availability and eligibility are determined by individual project requirements. Not all registered participants will qualify for all surveys. Compensation is only awarded for surveys you are eligible for and complete according to project requirements.

3. Compensation

Compensation amounts are set per survey project. KenyaSurvey does not guarantee any specific income. Compensation is paid via M-Pesa to your registered number after verification requirements are met.

4. Account Verification

Before payment services are activated, participants must complete an account verification process. This confirms your registered mobile number and activates the M-Pesa payout channel. Verification fees are non-refundable.

5. Prohibited Conduct

Creating multiple accounts, submitting false information, or attempting to manipulate survey responses may result in account suspension and forfeiture of compensation.

6. Termination

KenyaSurvey reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect your phone number, PIN (hashed), demographic information (name, county, occupation, education), and survey responses.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to match you to relevant surveys, process compensation payments, and maintain platform security. Demographic data may be shared in aggregate, anonymised form with research clients.

M-Pesa Payments

Your phone number is used to process M-Pesa payments. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN. Payment processing is handled by Safaricom's M-Pesa API.

Data Security

All passwords and PINs are hashed. Connections are encrypted via HTTPS. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting support@kenyasurvey.com.
