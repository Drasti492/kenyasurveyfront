Process

How It Works

Joining KenyaSurvey is straightforward. From registration to compensation, here is what the process looks like.

1

Create a Participant Account

Register with your Kenyan mobile number and set a secure PIN. Your phone number is used for identity verification and M-Pesa compensation.

2

Complete Your Participant Profile

Provide your demographic information — county, occupation, and education level. This helps us match you with research projects relevant to your profile.

3

Participate in Available Research Surveys

Access surveys relevant to your demographic profile. Answer all questions honestly — research quality depends on accurate responses.

4

Compensation is Calculated Per Eligible Response

Compensation varies by project, survey length and eligibility. Not all surveys pay the same amount.

5

Complete Account Verification

Before your first payment, a one-time account verification process confirms your registered mobile number and activates M-Pesa payout services.

6

Submit a Payment Request

Once verified, submit payment requests to your registered M-Pesa number. A 4% processing fee applies. Processing may take up to 24 hours.

About Compensation

Compensation varies depending on the project, survey length and your eligibility. KenyaSurvey does not guarantee a specific income. A 4% processing fee applies to all payment requests. Payments are processed via M-Pesa after account verification is complete.

Terms of Service

1. Eligibility

You must be 18 years or older and a resident of Kenya to participate. Multiple accounts are not permitted.

2. Survey Participation

Survey availability and eligibility are determined by individual project requirements. Compensation is only awarded for surveys you are eligible for and complete according to project requirements.

3. Compensation & Fees

Compensation amounts are set per survey project. A 4% processing fee applies to all payment requests. KenyaSurvey does not guarantee any specific income.

4. Account Verification

Before payment services are activated, participants must complete a one-time account verification. Verification fees are non-refundable.

5. Prohibited Conduct

Creating multiple accounts, submitting false information, or attempting to manipulate survey responses may result in account suspension and forfeiture of compensation.

6. Fraud

Any attempt to fraudulently obtain compensation will result in immediate account termination and may be reported to relevant authorities.
